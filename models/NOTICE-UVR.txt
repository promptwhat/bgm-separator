This browser application applies those published STFT and model parameters in
JavaScript and ONNX Runtime Web. It does not retrain or alter model weights.
The model estimates singing vocals; instrumental audio is mixture minus vocals.
Separation artifacts and retained instrument sounds remain possible. It does
not distinguish a lead singer from backing singers.
